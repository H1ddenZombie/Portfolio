Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this website handles information when you visit it or contact the publisher through the contact details provided on the site.

Overview

This is a static publisher and portfolio website for mobile applications by Rhys Sadler. It is used to provide public information about apps, support details, privacy policies, terms, and related publisher information.

Information collected

This website does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors through forms, account systems, or direct user registration.

If you contact the publisher by email, the information you choose to include in your message may be received and used only for the purpose of responding to your enquiry or support request.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking tools for behavioural profiling.

Basic technical processing may still occur through your browser, device, hosting provider, or GitHub Pages infrastructure in order to deliver the website normally.

Third-party links

This website may contain links to third-party services, including app store listings and external websites. If you follow those links, your data may be handled according to the privacy policies of those third parties.

How information is used

Any information voluntarily provided by email may be used only to:

  • Respond to support requests
  • Reply to business or partnership enquiries
  • Handle questions relating to apps, privacy, or terms

Data retention

Email correspondence may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to manage the relevant enquiry, support matter, or business communication.

Your choices

You are not required to submit personal information to browse this website. If you do not want your information shared by email, do not send it through the contact address listed on the site.
