Tally Spin Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tally Spin handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Tally Spin is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

  • No personally identifiable information is collected by the app.
  • The app does not require account creation or user login.

2. Advertising

Tally Spin may display ads provided by third-party services such as Google AdMob.

  • These services may collect anonymous data such as device information and usage data.
  • This data is used to provide and improve ad experiences.
  • Tally Spin does not control how third-party services collect or use data.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate and maintain the app
  • To improve performance and stability
  • To serve advertisements

4. Data Sharing

Tally Spin does not sell or share personal data. Any data collected by third-party services is handled according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

Tally Spin does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
